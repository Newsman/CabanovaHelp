The **Shape Elements**  are : **Circle, Square, Rounded Square, Polygon, Star, Gear, Wedge and Background bar** .

Elements that can be used to create a background field for other elements like images or text,to highlight some of your elements by putting them in a shape, or if you just want to decorate your page.

![shapes](legacy/iulv-5xid6dq5qi8f-lz48p.jpg)

All elements can change color, can change the width and hight unlimited, can have animations or effects like transparency, drop shadow or you can keep element fixed while scrolling the page, you can also link it to an external link, to a page withing this site, to an email, to download a file, to an action.

![all elements](legacy/iulv-63nyj7zzuspb-s16ya.jpg)

There are some **particularities** , you can edit them by pressing the **Properties**  button :

The **Rounded Square**  can, as the name says, **round**  the corners.

![Rounded Square](legacy/iulv-7w8hxe1qsx5-dwt10.jpg)

The **Polygon**  can change the number of **sides**  and the **angle** .

![Polygon](legacy/iulv-e8pzf82edih-yt6hb.jpg)

The **Star**  can change the number of **Corners** , can change the **Inner Radius**  and can change the **angle** .

![Star](legacy/iulv-h0aqrach695-y5km2.jpg)

The **Gear**  can change the number of **Sides** , can change the **Inner Radius** , can change the **Hole Radius**  or the **Hole Sides**  and can change the **angle** .

![Gear](legacy/iulv-nnrgp1sonop-eu6za.jpg)

The **Wedge**  can change the **Start Angle**  and the **Arc** .

![Wedge](legacy/iulv-st16g49ym7d-qe2dc.jpg)

The **Background bar**  have a preset width that covers the whole page, leaving only the hight to be edited, also it doesn't have the **Link**  option.

![Background bar](legacy/iulv-ybbi5lq7js9-qz4ml.jpg)
