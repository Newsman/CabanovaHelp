In the **Page**  ribbon **[1]**  you can find **Page password [2]** .

![page-password](legacy/iulv-1nev4lbmhrm1-9dajo.jpg)

If you click on it a window named **Page password**  will open. You can put a password on your page if you want only certain people to see that page.
