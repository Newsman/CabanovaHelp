In the **Page**  ribbon **[1]**  you have **Page HTML [2]**  option.

![page html](legacy/iulv-3uo9gos4gkku-2n1er.jpg)

If you click on it a window named **Page HTML**  will open.

![html window](legacy/iulv-3yfcsprok2zy-bafih.jpg)

You can insert your HTML code as a **header**  or **footer**  of your page.

To insert Header HTML click on **HTML above page [1]** .

![html above the page](legacy/iulv-59ox6n78rdn8-f1ix6.jpg)

To insert Footer HTML click on **HTML below page [2]** .

![html below the page](legacy/iulv-5gu7hvhg5iok-i6i6x.jpg)
