In the **Edit [1]**  ribbon there are the **Undo [2]**  and **Redo [3]**  buttons, they are also permanent in the editing tool bar: the **undo**  is an **arrow to left [4]**  and **redo**  is the **arrow to right [5]** . The **Undo**  button will move back an action and the **Redo**  button will restore your "**Undo** " actions.
