**Facebook** , **Twitter** , **Google Plus**  and **Share Page**  are the social media elements.

To insert facebook elements go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Facebook [2]** . With **Facebook**  option you can add:

![social-media](legacy/iulv-21v1g0vq4sca-pictz.jpg)

**Like Button**  - The Like button lets a user share your content with friends on Facebook.

**Like Box**  - The Like Box is a social plugin that enables Facebook Page owners to attract and gain Likes from their own website. Please note, this Like Box cannot be used for personal pages.

**Comments**  - The Comments Box easily enables your users to comment on your site's content.

**Activity Feed**  - The Activity Feed plugin displays the most interesting recent activity taking place on your site.

**Facepile**  - The Facepile plugin displays the Facebook profile pictures of users who have liked your page.

**Recommendatitons**  - The Recommendations plugin shows personalized recommendations to your users.

To add twitter element go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Twitter [2]** . With **Twitter**  option you can add:

![twitter](legacy/iulv-1jhw6abkh7h6-xghdf.jpg)

**Tweet Button**  - Add this button to your website to let people share content on Twitter without having to leave the page.

**Profile Widget**  - Display your most recent Twitter updates on any webpage.

To add google plus element go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Google Plus [2]** . With **Google Plus**  option you can add:

![Google Plus](legacy/iulv-3t1ic6tnq7a2-tzp87.jpg)

**Google Plus One Button**  - Add this button to your website to let people share content on Google Plus without having to leave the page.

**Google Plus Profile Badge**  - The Google+ Profile Badge helps visitors to your web site connect with you on Google+.

**Google Plus Page Badge**  - The Google+ Page Badge helps you get more recommendations for your site in Google search and grow your audience on Google+.

To add a share page element go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Share Page [2]** . With **Share Page**  option you can share your page in the following social enviroments:

**Google+, Twitter, Formspring.me, Blogger, Facebook, MySpace, Digg, Linkedln, Delicious, Google Bookmarks, StumbleUpon**
