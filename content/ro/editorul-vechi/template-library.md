If you don't have a picture of how you want your site to look like, we have more than 300 templates for you to choose and edit them as you like.

![template library](legacy/iulv-39vqz6ak7fcj-5hniy.jpg)

**Note:**  You don't have to use a template if you don't want, you can build your site from scratch. To do that just close the pop-up window "**Templates** " and you will get a blank page.
