To hide a page from the navigation menu, go to **page options [1]**  and select "**Hide page in navigation** " **[2]** .

![Hide nav](legacy/iulv-2r98yjrbdvjr-7ba1o.jpg)

There will apear a check mark in the box next to it and the page won't be displayed in navigation.
