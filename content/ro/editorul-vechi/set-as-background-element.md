You can add elements such as images or shapes and set them as background for other elements. To do that follow the steps:

First add the element you want as **background [1]**  and move it under the element that **need a background [2]** .

![Mobile background one](legacy/iulv-gifqf6xaa7u-xkv45.jpg)

And second go to **Set element as background**  button **[3]**  and click on **Set as background for previous element [4]** . The output will be the two elements combined **[5]** with one as background.

![Mobile background two](legacy/iulv-pppw2td4md6-kcrvk.jpg)
