In the **Mobile Panel**  you have the option to **Float**  an element to **left**  or **right** . To do that click on **Float icon [1]**  and click on **Float element to the left [2]**  or **Float element to the right [3]** , you can also remove the floating by clicking on **Remove float [4]** .
