To "**Paste in place** " an element, copy the element and go to "**Edit** " **[1]**  ribbon and click on "**Paste in place** " **[2]** .

![Paste in place](legacy/iulv-3jvath40kpf4-7iv7e.jpg)

This is useful when you need to move an element from a page to another but keep the same position.
