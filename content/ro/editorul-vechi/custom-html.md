Here you can enter your **custom HTML code** . For example, banner codes and embed codes for widgets from other websites should be entered here. This content will be displayed in your **published website only** . To insert a custom HTML go to Insert ribbon and click on Custom HTML.

![custom html](legacy/iulv-4q142u3gcssu-hrazg.jpg)

To do that go to **Configure [1]**  in editing tool bar and enter your in HTML code in the "**Custom html** " **[2]**  box.
