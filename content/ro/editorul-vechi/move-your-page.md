You can move your page in more ways, you can move it up and down in your page list, or you can make it a **child(subpag)**  from a **parrent(page)**  and the other way around.

To move a page select the page and go to **page option [1]**  at "**Move page** " **[2]** .

Here you have 2 sets of options, move **up [3]**  or move **down [4]** , and move **up a level [5]**  or move **down a level[6]** .

![move page](legacy/iulv-38cphjquagr7-pwyk8.jpg)

You cand add subpages to other **subpages**  making them **sub-subpages**  and so on.

![Sub-subpage](legacy/iulv-1mpfvut6y7kd-qgenx.jpg)

You can also move your pages with "**drag and drop** " option. To do that just hold left click on a **page [1]**  and **move it where you want [2]** .

![drag and drop](legacy/iulv-3fd6goj3aez7-snpg4.jpg)
