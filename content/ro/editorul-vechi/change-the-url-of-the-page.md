First, why would you want to change the **URL of your page** ? That's helping with the **SEO**  score and will make your page easier to find in search engines.

To change the URL of the page, go to **page option [1]**  and select "**Page URL** " **[2]** .

![page-url](legacy/iulv-tz34eiok64g-8qj4s.jpg)

There you will have a pop-up window with a field to change from "**undefined.html** " **[3]**  to your desired name (please keep in mind that this affects your SEO, choose a URL name that matches the content of the page).

![pop-up](legacy/iulv-6x6dw3p69mlr-swq12.jpg)
