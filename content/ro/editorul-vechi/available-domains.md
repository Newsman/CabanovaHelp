Any of the following extensions can be registered with Cabanova:

![list of domains](legacy/iulv-75ek09jd3auq-njb47.jpg)

To check the availability of the domain of your choice and place an order for it:

- Log into your Cabanova account

- Click on '**Add a custom Domain** ' **[1]**  at the Website package section level you'd like to associate the domain with

![domain-email](legacy/iulv-6qjdtxiv9lam-dp34d.jpg)

- Enter the domain **[2]**  you wish to register - without the extension 'www.' in the 'www.' Field

- Then select the extension **[3]**  of your choice ('com','net',...) from the available dropdown list and click on 'Check domain' **[4]**

![enter domain](legacy/iulv-2hk45bfmyf1v-l1cay.jpg)

- If the domain is available, hit '**Regiser Domain** ' **[1]** , choose registration duration for it (1, 2 or 5 Years) **[2]**  then follow the instructions on the screen to complete the order.

![select](legacy/iulv-4rvswncbuvis-t9ksp.jpg)

![Untitled](legacy/iulv-587nuqwm2t5g-g85ol.jpg)

The domain will be registered and activated within maximum 48 hours.
