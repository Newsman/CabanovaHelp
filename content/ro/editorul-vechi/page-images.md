In the **SEO [1]**  ribbon you have **Page Images [2]** .

![image-alt-text](legacy/iulv-120hd1irfw29-vwjul.jpg)

If you click on it a window named **SEO Images [3]**  will open. Here you have a list with all the **Images [4]**  from the page and to the right you can edit the **Alt Text [5]**  to help the search engines figure out what that picture is about.

![seo-images](legacy/iulv-34jdoshff3nq-yifxc.jpg)
