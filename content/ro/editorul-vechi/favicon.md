A "**Favicon** " is a special icon associated with your website, displayed in the browser's address bar.

To add a "**Favicon** " there are 2 simple steps:

**1.** Go to "**Template** " **[1]**  and click on "**Favicon** " **[2]** .

![favicon](legacy/iulv-7algkc1fhbs9-rllxz.jpg)

**2.** Next click on "**Upload** " **[3]**  and select your photo. After you see your photo uploaded you can close the window.

![Favicon](legacy/iulv-55wcoh5xaaho-d5k7f.jpg)

**Note:**  To remove a "**Favicon** " go back to Favicon window and pres the red "**X** ".

![redx](legacy/iulv-3qp7d2bncph7-ior9t.jpg)
