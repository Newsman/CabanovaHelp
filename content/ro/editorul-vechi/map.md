You have the option to add a **Google Map**  on your website. To do that go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Map [2]** .

![Map](legacy/iulv-3vqw1u3nhxna-dtpkm.jpg)

You can select a place to be **pined**  on the map by clicking on the **Map Settings [1]**  in the editing tool bar and entering the **address [2]**  of your **location** .

![map settings](legacy/iulv-3y2cwa51ue1i-enyy7.jpg)
