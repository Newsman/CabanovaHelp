If you already own a domain and it is hosted at a different provider, you can transfer it to Cabanova. Our regular [**domain subscription fees**](https://www.cabanova.com/p/en/domains.html) apply, but the actual transfer of your domain to Cabanova is FREE of charge.

**Before you order your domain transfer, please check below to see what you need prepare..COM / .NET / .ORG / .INFO / .BIZ / .ASIA / .NAME / .MOBI**

The domain must have been registered at or transferred to your current provider at least 60 days before you can transfer it to Cabanova.

Please contact your current hosting provider and:

**1.**  Request for the domain to be unlocked for transfer.

**2.**  Retrieve the "Auth-Code" for the domain. The auth-code is a key that will allow us to transfer your domain to Cabanova, and you will be asked to enter it in the transfer process.

**.DE**

Please contact your current hosting provider and retrieve the "Auth-Code" for the domain. The auth-code is a key that will allow us to transfer your domain to Cabanova, and you will be asked to enter it in the transfer process.

**.AT**

During the transfer process, you will recieve an email from NIC.AT which will allow you to validate the transfer.

**.ES**

During the transfer process, you will receive an email from NIC.ES which will allow you to validate the transfer.

**.CH, .EU, .NL**

Please contact your current hosting provider and retrieve the "Auth-Code" for the domain. The auth-code is a key that will allow us to transfer your domain to Cabanova, and you will be asked to enter it in the transfer process.

**.RO**

After ordering your domain, you'll need to log into your [**ROTLD**](http://rotld.ro/) account and set up the domain with the following Nameservers:

Primary Nameserver: ns1.ns-serve.net

Secondary Nameserver: ns2.ns-serve.net

**.UK**

Please contact your current hosting provider and:

**1.**  Request for your domain IPSTAG to be changed to ANTAGUS-DE

**2.**  Send us a confirmation email once the IPSTAG change is done to support@cabanova.com

**.BE1.**  Go to http://www.dns.be/whois/

**2.**  At the WHOIS section, enter your domain and hit "Whois"

**3.**  Click on "I Agree" and scroll down to the bottom of the page

**4.**  Click on "Yes" next to "Do you as a registrant of "yourdomain.de" wish to request a transfercode?"

**5.** You'll receive an email with the "Auth code" of your domain on the email address your domain is associated with.

**6.**  The auth-code is a key that will allow us to transfer your domain to Cabanova, and you will be asked to enter it in the transfer process.
