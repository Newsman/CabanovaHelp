Welcome to**Google - Cabanova Search Engine Optimization Guide**

The Cabanova Search Engine Optimization Guide is a simplified version of the Google SEO Starter Guide, of which the complete version can be downloaded [**here**](files/downloads/cabanova-google-seo-guide-en.pdf).

The Google SEO Starter Guide has been simplfiied to help you optimize your Cabanova Website for search engines. Except sections with referral to the **Cabanova Sitebuilder** , which were built in separately from the core content, other sections remain mostly unchanged as they are irrelevant to the SEO process of your Cabanova Website since we have them handled automatically for you.

Each topic of the guide - except those which require no intervention at the Website level - is structured as follows:

**1.**  Topic overview by Google SEO Starter Guide - with slight changes where required.

**2.**  How to apply the provided advices to your Cabanova Website.

**3.**  Best Practices.
