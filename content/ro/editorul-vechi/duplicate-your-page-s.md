This tool is useful when you want to keep the structure of certain pages of your site.

To **Duplicate**  a page, go to **page options [1]**  select the page you want to copy and select "**Duplicate Page** " **[2]** .

![dupli](legacy/iulv-2xd8w49c7y0n-tsjgj.jpg)

Another page with the same content will apear on your site, it will have the same name as the original plus the word "**Copy** " **[3]**  next to it.

![dupli copy](legacy/iulv-4hbgs0ii9an3-vd4qp.jpg)
