Here you can create a **Guestbook**  to let people share their opinion about your site. To insert a guestbook, go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **Guestbook [2]** .

![guestbook insert](legacy/iulv-72vg3c0pc18i-325cl.jpg)

You can select the **email address**  where you'd like to receive notifications, by default is the account email address, you can choose if you want to see the **entries**  to approve them or not, and you can change the **Sign Guestbook Button** .

![guestbook new](legacy/iulv-61fjknzvpev0-ea6uk.jpg)

You can remove entries at any time from your guest book, go to **Setting** , **Entries**  and pres the button for the unwanted comment.

![redx](legacy/iulv-3qp7d2bncph7-ior9t.jpg)

![Guestbook remove](legacy/iulv-3rdaeb7lwxw3-a7ktt.jpg)
