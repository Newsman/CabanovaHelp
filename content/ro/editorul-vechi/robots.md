In the **SEO [1]**  ribbon you have **Robots [2]** . "**Robots** " or "**crawlers** " are the names for what search engine uses to find information about your page, the **robots.txt**  file will help search engines find content on your site easier, or will tell them not to look at some parts of your site if you so choose.

![robots](legacy/iulv-o7lpzcvho8c-iaqy2.jpg)

If you click on **Robots** , a window name **Robots [3]**  will open. Here you can **Upload [4]** , **Remove [5]**  or **Download [6]**  your robots.txt file. You can aslo see the **content [7]**  of your file.

![robots](legacy/iulv-8on583drpkl-rg4hp.jpg)
