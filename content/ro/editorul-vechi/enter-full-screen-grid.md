In the **View [1]**  ribbon you have the **Grid [2]**  and **Enter full screen [3]**  buttons.

![layout-grid](legacy/iulv-djknzwpaf9l-5m09a.jpg)

A grid is used to help you arrange the items on your page better, you can set a **Light grid [1]**  or a **Dark grid [2]**  and you can make them **Small [3]** , **Medium [4]**  or **Large [5]** . To turn off the grid click on **Grid Off [6]** .

![grid-style](legacy/iulv-2tpcemwiy9d3-axa42.jpg)
