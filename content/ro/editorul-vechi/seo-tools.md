In the **SEO [1]**  ribbon you have **Page Metadata [2]** , if you click on it a window named **Page Metadata [3]**  will open and you can edit your **Page Title [4]** , you can write a **Short Description [5]** , and you can edit the **Keywords [6]** .

![seo-page-metadata](legacy/iulv-5likxv4fdz7r-27c2q.jpg)

Remember to put a **page title** , **short description**  and **keywords**  that matches the **content**  of that page otherwise the **search engines**  will avoid your website.
