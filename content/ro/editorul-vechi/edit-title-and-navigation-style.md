You can edit your mobile title and navigation, in Mobile Panel click on **Settings [1]**  and a window named **Mobile Site Settings [2]**  will open. Here you have a **Text box [3]**  with the mobile site name, you can Align the **Top bar [4]**  in 6 different ways, you can change the background of the **Content and Navigation [5]** , and you can also edit the text style of the **Title [6]**  and **Navigation [7]** . The text in content area keeps the configuration from the website.
