In order to keep your changes on your website you need the save button. In the **File [1]**  ribbon you can **Save draft [2]** , witch will save all your changes but it will **not apply**  them to your online version of the site. Or you can **Publish online [3]**  to make your site live and let others see it, the publish online button will **save and apply**  your changes.
