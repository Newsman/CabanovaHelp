In **View [1]**  ribbon you have two options: **View draft site [2]**  and **View published site [3]** .

![draft-view](legacy/iulv-3vtr79qqctw1-rktdr.jpg)

The first one (**View draft site** ) will show a draft of your site with all your saved changes, and the second (**View published site** ) will display your live website. When you look in address bar you will notice that a draft site has "/draft" at the end while a live website doesn't.

![new draft](legacy/iulv-6m0uxxpx5iqq-mbixs.jpg)

The difference is that you can edit your website in **draft** , make and save changes, and they will not be displayed in the live website until you **publish**  those changes. If you click on [**Publish website**](../manage-my-website/), all the changes up to that point will be saved and the site will go live.
