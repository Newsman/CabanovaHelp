To edit the Template, go to "**Template** " **[1]**  and check the "**Edit Template** " **[2]**  box.

![Edit Template](legacy/iulv-4fvuzycoo58h-js29d.jpg)

Once you finish editing the **template [1]** , click on "**Close Template** " **[3]** , or click again the "**Edit Template** " **[2]**  box. Both methods go back to first page of your site.

![Close](legacy/iulv-4iaic6jjejcx-f8fu8.jpg)

![Close template](legacy/iulv-4p6c05ir2xdt-u9ymw.jpg)

Any element that is inserted in "**Template mode** " will be visible throughout the entire site.

In template mode you can edit the [**width and hight**](../width-and-hight/) for your site, you can add [**predefined templates**](../template-library/) and [**predefined pages**](../predefined-pages/) and edit them as you like.

You can also add a "[**Favicon**](../favicon/)", a favicon is a special icon associated with your website, displayed in the browser's address bar.
