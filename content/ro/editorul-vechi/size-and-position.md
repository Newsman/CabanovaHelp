**Size and position**  can be edited in two ways:

**1.**  The size can be change by holding **left click on the handles [1]**  of and element and draging them in the direction you wish. And the position can be changed with "**Drag and drop** ", wich means you can **hold left click [2]**  on an element and move it where you want.

![handles](legacy/iulv-4rschk61busd-p0p0t.jpg)

![drag and drop](legacy/iulv-7ayrgcs9jxjx-kopyj.jpg)

**2.**  Go to **Edit [1]**  ribbon and click on **Size and Position [2]** , make sure you have the element to be edited selected. Here you have a better way to edit size and position: **"X:" [3]**  is the vertical coordonate and **"Y:" [4]**  is the horizontal coordonate , **"W:" [5]**  is the width of the element and **"H:" [6]**  is the hight of the element. You also have **arrows [7]**  for better precision in moving your elements.
