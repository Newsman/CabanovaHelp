You can create a **Poll**  to ask people their opinion about anything you want. To add a poll go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and select **Poll [2]** .

You can add multiple polls each with it's own question and answers.

![poll insert](legacy/iulv-64mhntk43sp7-b763u.jpg)

In the **Poll Settings**  menu, you can edit your **question [1]**  and **answers [2]** . There is no limit for how many answers you can have but it's recommended to keep it short and accurate.

![Poll settings](legacy/iulv-6cldv6kksuh1-oel3u.jpg)
