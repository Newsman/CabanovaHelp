In **Edit [1]**  ribbon you can find **Duplicate [2]** , **Select All [3]**  and **Deselect [4]** .

![duplicate select deselect](legacy/iulv-58g9b1kgt2u6-ghb3f.jpg)

**Duplicate**  option will make a copy of your selected element right next to it.

**Select All**  will select all the elements in the page, you can also select manually by holding left click and creating a square surrounding the element you want. This helps when you want to copy/delete or simply move a group of elements without changing the spacing between them. Deselect will remove the current selection.
