In the **Mobile Panel** , you have the option to set the **padding**  of an element. This is useful when you want to arrange your elements. To acces **padding** click on the **padding icon [1]**  and a window named **Padding [2]**  will open. Here you can set the **Top** , **Bottom** , **Right**  and **Left**  padding.
