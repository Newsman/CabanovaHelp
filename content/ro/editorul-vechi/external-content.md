You can add **entire HTML pages**  within your site with the **External Content**  element. To do that go to **Insert [1]**  ribbon and click on **External Content [2]** .

![External content](legacy/iulv-5q1m9f848pyv-j74ju.jpg)

To edit the page you want to display go to **Configure [1]**  in the editing tool bar and insert the URL to your **external content [2]** .

![external editing](legacy/iulv-pzuzua6wq1f-sf8cv.jpg)

**NOTE: Very immportant, make sure you are not breaking any copyrights when you are doing so.**
