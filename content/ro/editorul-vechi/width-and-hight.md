With the new touch editor, now your page have unlimited **hight [1]**  and **width [2]** (though we recommend **not**  to increase your page width and hight to extreme sizes, for esthetic purpose).

![Capture](legacy/iulv-4kxdnsfr4amr-nau8h.jpg)

**Note:**  Keep in mind that your site will become wider only to the right.

This affects how the site will be visible on phones and tablets. When you open a non-mobile site on your phone, it will be displayed starting from the margin limit on the left.
