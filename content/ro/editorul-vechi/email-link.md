To link an element to an email, select the element you want to link, clik on **link icon [1]** , select **Email Link [2]** , insert the **email address [3]**  (you can insert your account email by clicking on the **Default address [4]** ). Also you can insert a predefined **Subject [5]**  and predefined **Initial content [6]** , both of them are optional.
